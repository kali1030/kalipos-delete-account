# Account Deletion

**Effective Date:** June 5, 2026

DailyPOS allows users to request deletion of their account directly inside the app.

## How to Delete Your Account

1. Open DailyPOS.
2. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
3. Open the account or settings section.
4. Choose the delete account option.
5. Follow the confirmation steps in the app to permanently delete the account.

## What Happens When You Delete Your Account

When you delete your account from inside DailyPOS:

- Your Firebase Authentication account may be deleted
- Your cloud backup data stored for that account may be deleted
- Account-linked app data associated with that account may be removed

## What May Remain

Some information may remain in the following cases:

- Data stored locally on your device may remain until you uninstall the app or clear app storage
- Information you shared to third-party apps such as WhatsApp is controlled by those services after sharing
- Certain technical, billing, or legal records may be retained where required for fraud prevention, dispute handling, or legal compliance
